FAQ

Answers to
Your Queries

What is your typical project timeline?
+
Simple tasks like social edits take 1–2 weeks. Full branding or video campaigns typically run 3–6 weeks depending on scope and revisions.
What services does Zerru Edge offer?
+
We specialise in Video Editing, Graphic Design, Art Direction, Motion Graphics, Brand Identity, and Visual Strategy for digital platforms.
What is your revision policy?
+
Every project includes 2 rounds of revisions. Additional rounds can be added at a flat hourly rate. We aim to nail it in the first pass with thorough briefing.
Will I own the rights to the designs?
+
Absolutely. Upon final payment, full intellectual property rights are transferred to you. We retain the right to showcase work in our portfolio unless stated otherwise.
How do we get started?
+
Hit the Contact Us button, fill in your brief, and we'll schedule a discovery call within 48 hours. From there we scope the project and send a detailed proposal.
